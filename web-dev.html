artist portfolio for Shareeka Helaluddin

one of my favourite things about working with artists is they usually have a very strong vision for their site, and the collaborative process of bringing that to life is super fun. Nosebleed

Nosebleed is a tv-guide style website for independent film screenings in sydney. The editor Amelia Broughton first approached me with the idea back in April and I worked with her closely to develop the concept, design, and site. Solo developed by me, it was also my first time using Kirby CMS to build a back end too. this was an absolute joy to work on, definitely one of my favourite projects so far.

--nosebleed.sydney

Business site for Mikhaela Rodwell

Mikhaela Rodwell is many things to many people. she has a wide skillset, ripe for application to a very specific market. as such, her website was authentic, professional and dedicated. tasteful, but not indulgent. all the images in the "gallery" are drawn straight from her camera roll: an earnest invitation into her expertise and the incisive way she navigates both culture and business.

--mikhaelarodwell.com
