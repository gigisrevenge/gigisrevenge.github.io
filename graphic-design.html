Runway Journal x Worms Issue 50: Correspondence

Volunteering at Runway is first and foremost a huge honour and I really cherish the chance to give back to the arts community here in so-called australia. I also love any opportunity to challenge myself and learn something new so for the Issue 50 cover I really wanted to give 3d javascript a go. I thought a lot about Worm's tagline “fertilise your mind”, which is so simple but connotes so much - the Runway version would be something like “fertilise your desktop”, equally simple and playful, inviting and also challenging. so that's what the cover is trying to channel; it's an invitation to move around a silly, experimental place that pushes the limits of what both writing and web art can be. Text by fellow Runway board member Amy Yu.
