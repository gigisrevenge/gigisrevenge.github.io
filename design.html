Nosebleed

Nosebleed is a tv-guide style website for independent film screenings in sydney. The editor Amelia Broughton first approached me with the idea back in April and I worked with her closely to develop the concept, design, and site. Solo developed by me, it was also my first time using Kirby CMS to build a back end too. this was an absolute joy to work on, definitely one of my favourite projects so far.

--nosebleed.sydney
